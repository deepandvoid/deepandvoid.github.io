Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Deep and Void Pubblica ("the app") is a private tool used by one creator to publish their own videos on their own YouTube, TikTok and Instagram accounts.

Data the app accesses

  • Basic account information (account or channel name and id), only to choose the right channel.
  • Permission to upload videos and set their title, description, tags, thumbnail and publication time.
  • Read-only statistics of the creator's own videos (views, watch time), shown only to the creator.
  • Read-only access to the public comments on the creator's own videos, used once a week to show the creator the questions viewers ask. The app never posts, edits or deletes comments.

The app does not read private messages, contacts, subscriber lists or any data from other channels. It does not build profiles of viewers.

How data is stored

Access tokens are stored only on the creator's own computer. Nothing is sent to any server other than the official APIs of Google (YouTube), TikTok and Meta (Instagram). No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Google user data

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses YouTube API Services and is bound by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Data retention and deletion

The app stores only what the creator needs, on the creator's own computer: the access tokens, short reports with the statistics of the creator's own videos, and a weekly list of viewer questions taken from comments (comment text only, no usernames). Reports and lists older than 30 days are deleted, and statistics are refreshed from the API at least every 30 days. Access tokens are kept until access is revoked and can be deleted at any time by deleting the local token files. On request to the contact below, any stored data is deleted within 7 days.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google account permissions page (myaccount.google.com/permissions), from TikTok's "Manage app permissions" settings, or from Instagram's "Apps and websites" settings.
